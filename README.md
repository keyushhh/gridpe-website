## ⚡ Highlights

- **Doorstep Cash Delivery**: Request currency in exact denominations directly to your door.
- **Transparent Fee Structure**: Upfront pricing breakdown before confirmation — delivery fee, platform fee, and GST on fees only (GST never touches the cash).
- **Zero Locked Balances**: Pay per delivery. No wallets, deposits, or hidden minimum balances.
- **Trust & Verification**:
  - KYC-verified delivery partners.
  - Biometric & MPIN security.
  - 6-digit OTP-confirmed handover.
  - Full refund protection for unfulfilled or cancelled deliveries.
- **Foreign Currency Exchange**: Exchange major global currencies (USD, EUR, GBP) at clear, transparent rates without inflated airport markups.
- **Coverage Map**: Live pilot in Bengaluru with upcoming expansions across India.

## 🛠️ Architecture & Stack

- **Frontend**: Semantic HTML5 & Modern Vanilla CSS3 (Custom Fluid Typography, 3D kinetic animations, dark aesthetic).
- **Interactions**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) with custom scroll triggers, kinetic type, vector animations, and interactive SVG mapping.
- **Performance**: Zero external UI framework overhead; sub-second load times and production-optimized WebP asset pipelines.
- **SEO & Discoverability**: Full OpenGraph protocol support, semantic hierarchy, and JSON-LD structured schema markup.
