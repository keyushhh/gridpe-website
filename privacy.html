Privacy Policy

About this website

The policy below covers the Grid.Pe app and platform. This website (grid.pe) is deliberately lighter: the only thing you can hand us here is your email address, and only if you type it into the waitlist form and submit it. That email is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and used to send you launch and availability updates for your city. Nothing else on this page collects it.

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, session recording or cross-site analytics. Our hosting provider logs standard request data (IP address, user agent, referring page and timestamp) as part of serving the page. To be removed from the waitlist, write to hello@gridpe.app and we will delete your address.

1. Introduction

At Grid.Pe, your privacy is a priority. Grid.Pe is owned and operated as a Sole Proprietorship. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you utilize our hyperlocal technology application, platform interface, and delivery aggregation services. By accessing or using Grid.Pe, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • a. Personal Identification Information: Name, mobile number, date of birth, and identity documentation uploaded for user verification purposes (such as Permanent Account Number (PAN) or other officially valid documents). For international travelers utilizing our premium Multi-Currency or Foreign Exchange fulfillment section, this strictly includes passport data and visa scans.
  • b. Digital Payment Metadata: Bank account information, UPI IDs, and tokenized payment identifiers required to synchronize transactions with authorized, licensed payment processors.
  • c. Logistics & Device Analytics: Hyperlocal geographic location data (for real-time delivery routing and coordination), application interaction logs, device identifiers, and operating system specifications.

3. How We Use Your Data

We utilize collected information exclusively to:

  • Facilitate verification processing and maintain adherence to regulatory identity verification standards.
  • Process, track, and route on-demand hyperlocal delivery requests.
  • Provide real-time operational status communication and interactive timeline feedback.
  • Investigate, prevent, and mitigate unauthorized activities, payment discrepancies, or fraudulent transactions.

4. Data Storage & System Security

Your operational data is stored utilizing secure infrastructure configurations. We enforce industry-standard transmission safeguards, access controls, and encryption protocols. Sensitive card parameters (such as CVV codes) are never processed or retained on our independent infrastructure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not trade or sell user profiles. Information is shared strictly with:

  • Licensed, RBI-authorized Payment Gateways and banking operators processing your checkout transactions.
  • Secure identity verification networks and API nodes (e.g., identity engines for international passports).
  • Authorized enforcement bodies or regulatory authorities when compelled strictly under applicable laws.

6. Your Rights

You possess the right to review, update, or request the closure of your profile. To enforce these administrative preferences, contact our operations desk at hello@gridpe.app.

7. Data Retention

We store transaction records and identity verification metadata only as long as functionally necessary to fulfill standard corporate record-keeping requirements and comply with applicable regulatory guidelines.

8. Children’s Privacy

Platform access is strictly restricted to individuals aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly compile records from minors.

9. Policy Amendments

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy. Structural changes will be broadcast directly via the platform UI or electronic mail alerts.

10. Contact & Grievance Execution

For administrative questions or data clarifications, connect with our privacy officer at: hello@gridpe.app
