Straight answers

The awkward questions

Grid.Pe delivers cash. We are not a bank, and we are careful about the difference. Here is exactly what that means for your money.

Do you hold my money?

No. You pay for a single cash delivery at the moment you order it, the same way you pay for anything else. There is no Grid.Pe balance sitting between you and us.

Is it safe to open my door?

Every delivery partner passes an identity check before they can carry cash. You see their photo, name and KYC status in the app, and the handover only closes when you read out your six-digit OTP.

What does it cost me?

Every charge is on screen before you pay: a delivery fee that scales with the amount, a platform fee, and GST on those two. For currency conversion, a stated markup over the base rate.

Do I have to do KYC?

Yes, once, in the app. It is what sets your delivery limits, and it is the same reason we can be sure the cash reaches the right hands.

Are you a bank?

No. Grid.Pe is not a bank. We do not lend, we do not invest, and we do not hold deposits. We move cash and currency, and we work with licensed partners for the regulated parts of that.

What if something goes wrong?

Cancel any time before a partner is assigned, and free within 30 seconds of ordering. If a delivery fails, the payment is refunded in full, and support sits one tap away inside the order.
